Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to your most common questions.

🍽️

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes! Walk-ins are always welcome, though we recommend booking ahead on weekends to guarantee your table.

🌱

Do you have vegan options?

Absolutely! We offer a dedicated vegetarian and vegan menu. Just let your server know and we'll guide you.

🚚

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We deliver within a 10-mile radius. You can place orders via our website or popular delivery apps.

🎂

Can you cater private events?

We'd love to! Contact us with event details and we'll put together a custom catering package for you.

🌶️

Can I customise spice levels?

Of course! Most dishes can be adjusted from mild to extra spicy. Just inform your server when ordering.

💳

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. No surcharges applied.
