Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

🥗

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Absolutely! Over 40% of our menu is vegetarian or vegan-friendly, and we clearly label every dish. Just ask your server for guidance.

📅

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking for groups of 4+ or on weekends. Call us or use the form above.

🌶️

Can I adjust the spice level?

Yes! We offer 5 spice levels for most dishes — from mild to volcanic 🌋. Just mention your preference when ordering.

🚚

Do you deliver?

We deliver within a 15-mile radius. Average delivery time is 30 minutes. Minimum order for delivery is $20.

🎂

Can you cater private events?

Yes! We offer bespoke catering packages for birthdays, weddings, and corporate events. Contact us at least 5 days in advance.

🅿️

Is parking available?

Free street parking is available on Spice Street. There's also a public car park 2 minutes away on Flavour Avenue.
